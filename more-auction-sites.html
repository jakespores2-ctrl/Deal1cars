Before you bid on any auction

  • Read the fees. Many sites add a buyer's premium and closing fees on top of the winning bid, so the real price is higher.
  • Assume "as-is." Most surplus sales have no warranty and no returns.
  • Check the title. Ask what paperwork you get, and whether liens or back taxes come with the item.
  • Verify the site. Type the address yourself. Fake auction sites copy real ones.
  • Know your limit. Set a maximum before you start, and use the auto loan calculator to see what a purchase does to your budget.

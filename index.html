Government surplus and eBay Motors auctions, searchable in one place

Deal1Cars gathers vehicle, boat and trailer auctions from GSA Auctions, the U.S. government's surplus sales site, and eBay Motors. Search by make, model, type, state, price, or distance from your ZIP code, then click through to the original listing to bid.

Government surplus vehicles are often sold by federal agencies after service and can include sedans, trucks, vans, buses, boats and trailers. Condition varies, so read each listing and inspect before you bid.

Deal1Cars is an independent site and is not affiliated with GSA, GSA Auctions or eBay. Listing details, prices and end times come from those sites and can change or be out of date, so always confirm on the original listing before you bid. Prices shown are the last bid seen at the daily update. Distances are approximate and measured from the center of each ZIP code. Some eBay links are affiliate links, which means this site may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you.

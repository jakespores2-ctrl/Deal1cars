Tips

  • Keyboard: digits, + - * / ^ ( ) ! %, Enter for equals, Backspace to delete and Esc to clear.
  • The answer updates as you type. Missing closing brackets are added for you.
  • Tap DEG or RAD to switch how sin, cos and tan read angles.
  • Ans is your last answer. Pressing an operator right after equals continues from it.
  • Powers are worked out before multiplying, so -2^2 gives -4. Writing 2π or 3(4+1) multiplies without a sign.
  • Tap an entry in History to bring its expression back for editing.

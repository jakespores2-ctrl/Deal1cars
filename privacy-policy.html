Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version

This website offers free calculators. You do not need an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details. The numbers you type into a calculator are worked out in your own browser and are not sent to us or stored by us.

Like most websites, this one is served by a hosting company, loads its fonts from Google, and may show advertising and count visits. Those parts can involve cookies and similar technology, and they are explained below.

What you enter into the calculators

The loan, tax, affordability and scientific calculators run entirely in your browser. The amounts and expressions you enter stay on your device. We do not receive them, save them or share them. Closing or refreshing the page clears them.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, your browser connects to the servers that deliver this site and its parts. Those servers can see technical details such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request and the time of the request.

  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs that include visitor IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. The text on this site is displayed with fonts loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when it does. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Area search and location

If you use the "Near ZIP code" search on the home page, the ZIP code you type is sent to a free third-party lookup service (zippopotam.us) to find its map position. If you press "Use my location," your browser asks for permission first, and your position is used only in your browser to sort and filter listings. We do not store your ZIP code or location.
